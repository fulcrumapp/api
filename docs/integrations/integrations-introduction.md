## Integrating Fulcrum with other systems

Fulcrum is designed to work alongside the rest of your tools. You can use the [REST API](https://docs.fulcrumapp.com/reference/rest-api-intro) to create and update records and other objects, the [Query API](https://docs.fulcrumapp.com/reference/query-intro) to read data for analytics, and [webhooks](https://docs.fulcrumapp.com/docs/webhooks) to react to changes in real time.

### Use Cases

- Seed a Fulcrum app with existing GIS or enterprise data
- Keep Fulcrum records in sync with external systems on a schedule
- Trigger downstream actions when records are created or updated

The examples in this section walk through complete integrations you can adapt to your own workflows.
